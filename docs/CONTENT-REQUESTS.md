# Content requests for the doctors

Not deployed. Each service page needs ~700+ words; each doctor profile
~300+. Questions are written so each can be answered in a sentence or a
few bullet points. Reply inline or on a call; nothing publishes until
you approve the wording.

Conventions: "patients" not "cases"; plain language; Indian English.

## Services

### Viability scan

1. Earliest week you usually scan, and what you do when nothing is seen yet?
2. Transvaginal vs abdominal: which you use, when, and what the patient feels?
3. Exactly what you report on (location, sac, heartbeat, dating)?
4. What a patient should bring or do before the visit?
5. Common reasons the scan is inconclusive, and what happens next?
6. How long does it take at your centre?
7. Anything patients commonly worry about that you can reassure on?

### Down's screening (OSCAR)

1. Your exact window (we say 11 to 13+6 weeks — confirm)?
2. Which blood markers your lab measures?
3. What a "combined chance figure" looks like when you explain it to a patient (an example in words, not numbers you would not use)?
4. The three most common questions patients ask after a higher-chance result?
5. Whether results come same visit at your centre, or later?
6. What you advise about combining OSCAR with NIPT?

### First trimester anomaly scan

1. What this scan can and cannot detect at this stage (one line each)?
2. Which findings most often lead to a second-look scan later?
3. How it differs from OSCAR and from the 18-22 week anomaly scan in your words?
4. Who you specifically recommend it for?
5. Approximate duration and whether transvaginal views are sometimes needed?

### Anomaly scan

1. Your preferred window (we say 18-22 weeks)?
2. What you check that a routine scan does not cover — in your own order of importance?
3. How long a normal scan takes, and what happens if the baby is in a difficult position?
4. What patients receive at the end (report, images, explanation)?
5. The most common questions you hear during this scan?
6. Do partners/family attend, and any rules for the room?

### Growth scan with Doppler

1. Which Dopplers you routinely measure (uterine artery, umbilical, MCA, ductus venosus — tick which)?
2. When you start serial growth scans and how often?
3. Conditions that most often trigger a Doppler study at your centre?
4. What "abnormal Doppler" leads to in practice (monitoring, early delivery planning)?
5. Anything patients should know about report terms like AFI, EFW, centile?

### NIPT

2. Conditions screened on your standard panel (confirm what we can list)?
3. Your turnaround time and how results are communicated?

### Fetal echo

### Fetal neurosonogram

### Second opinion scan

### Genetic counselling

### Fetal postmortem

1. What the evaluation includes at your centre (imaging, external examination, tests — tick what applies)?

4. Turnaround for results and how they are shared?
5. Whether this is offered for losses at all gestations or from a specific stage?
6. How it connects to genetic counselling and future-pregnancy planning?

### Recurrent miscarriages clinic

Screening Scans

Ultrasound scans that check how your baby is growing and developing.

6 to 9 weeks

Viability Scan

An early pregnancy scan that confirms the pregnancy is in the right place and checks for your baby's heartbeat.

Learn more about Viability Scan
11 to 14 weeks

Down's Screening (OSCAR)

A combined first trimester screening that assesses the chance of Down's syndrome and other chromosomal conditions.

Learn more about Down's Screening (OSCAR)
11 to 14 weeks

First Trimester Anomaly Scan

A detailed early scan that checks your baby's development and can identify many structural conditions sooner.

Learn more about First Trimester Anomaly Scan
18 to 22 weeks

Anomaly Scan

The detailed mid-pregnancy scan that examines your baby's organs, growth, and wellbeing.

Learn more about Anomaly Scan
28 to 36 weeks

Growth Scan with Doppler

A scan that tracks your baby's growth and blood flow, especially important in the later months.

Learn more about Growth Scan with Doppler

Advanced Diagnostics

Detailed tests that look closer when more information is needed.

From 10 weeks

NIPT

A simple blood test from the mother that screens for common chromosomal conditions, with no risk to the baby.

Learn more about NIPT
18 to 24 weeks

Fetal Echo

A focused ultrasound of your baby's heart that checks its structure, function, and rhythm.

Learn more about Fetal Echo
When: on referral or as advised

Fetal Neurosonogram

A detailed scan of your baby's brain and spine, for conditions affecting the nervous system.

Learn more about Fetal Neurosonogram
When: on referral or as advised

Second Opinion Scan

A careful repeat scan and report review when you want reassurance or a specialist second opinion.

Learn more about Second Opinion Scan

Specialty Clinics

Focused care pathways for specific situations and conditions.

When: on referral or as advised

Genetic Counselling

In-house counselling that explains results, inheritance, and your options in clear language.

Learn more about Genetic Counselling
When: on referral or as advised

Fetal Postmortem

A specialist evaluation after pregnancy loss that helps families understand what happened and plan ahead.

Learn more about Fetal Postmortem
When: on referral or as advised

Recurrent Miscarriages Clinic

A clinic that investigates repeated pregnancy loss and plans care for your next pregnancy.

Learn more about Recurrent Miscarriages Clinic
When: on referral or as advised

Twin Specialised Clinic

Specialist monitoring and care for twin and multiple pregnancies.

Learn more about Twin Specialised Clinic

In keeping with the PCPNDT Act, 1994, none of our scans or services involve determination or disclosure of the sex of the fetus. This is a criminal offence in India and is never performed at this centre.

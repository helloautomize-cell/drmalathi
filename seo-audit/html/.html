Our services

The right scan, at the right week

Specialist scans and tests timed to your pregnancy, with findings explained in the same visit.

  • Viability6 to 9 weeks
  • NIPTFrom 10 weeks
  • OSCAR11 to 14 weeks
  • Anomaly18 to 22 weeks
  • Fetal echo18 to 24 weeks
  • Growth + Doppler28 to 36 weeks
6 to 9 weeks

Viability Scan

An early pregnancy scan that confirms the pregnancy is in the right place and checks for your baby's heartbeat.

Learn more about Viability Scan
From 10 weeks

NIPT

A simple blood test from the mother that screens for common chromosomal conditions, with no risk to the baby.

Learn more about NIPT
11 to 14 weeks

Down's Screening (OSCAR)

A combined first trimester screening that assesses the chance of Down's syndrome and other chromosomal conditions.

Learn more about Down's Screening (OSCAR)
18 to 22 weeks

Anomaly Scan

The detailed mid-pregnancy scan that examines your baby's organs, growth, and wellbeing.

Learn more about Anomaly Scan
18 to 22 weeks

Anomaly Scan

The detailed mid-pregnancy scan that examines your baby's organs, growth, and wellbeing.

Learn more about Anomaly Scan
18 to 24 weeks

Fetal Echo

A focused ultrasound of your baby's heart that checks its structure, function, and rhythm.

Learn more about Fetal Echo
28 to 36 weeks

Growth Scan with Doppler

A scan that tracks your baby's growth and blood flow, especially important in the later months.

Learn more about Growth Scan with Doppler

Your visit

What to expect at your visit

Every appointment follows the same flow, so you always know what comes next.

  1. 1

    Book

    Call, WhatsApp, or send a request online. Our team helps you pick the right scan for your stage.

  2. 2

    Scan by a specialist

    Your scan is performed or supervised by a fetal medicine specialist, not a technician alone.

  3. 3

    Your findings, explained straight away

    We walk you through what we see on the screen and what it means, in words that make sense.

  4. 4

    Report and plan

    You leave with your report and a clear next step. Complex findings are reviewed by our specialist panel if needed.

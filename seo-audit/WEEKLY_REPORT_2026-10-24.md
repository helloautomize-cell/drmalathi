## 3. Work completed (plain language)

- **Medical review now shows on every guide.** All 10 patient guides now say they are written and medically reviewed by the FMC Trichy clinical team, linked to the doctors page. Google now receives the correct "medically reviewed" markup for each guide.
- **Corrected the structured data on every page.** Each page now sends one connected data package naming the clinic, the page, its breadcrumbs and its FAQs — previously 16 disconnected fragments. Guides are marked as medical web pages about the actual condition or scan; clinics are no longer mislabelled as procedures.
- **Made the site one address only.** `www.fmctrichy.com` now permanently redirects to the plain domain so no duplicate version can be indexed.
- **Welcomed every major AI bot by name** in robots.txt (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google, Apple, Bing).
- **Added Editorial Policy and a new Medical Disclaimer page** to the footer (the page is drafted and awaits counsel review before it is indexed, like the other legal pages).
- **Doctor pages now list the patient guides on each doctor's areas of practice.**
- **Filled in the FAQs.** Services that had only 2–3 questions now have at least 4; 22 short answers across three guides were expanded to proper answer length; the NIPT guide gained its missing quick answer.
- **Added a Sources section to the 7 guides that had none**, citing ISUOG, SMFM, RCOG, NHS, WHO, NICE and the Fetal Medicine Foundation.
- **Removed the remaining machine-written fingerprints**: "Final Thoughts" headings on 8 guides and 25 title-case headings on 2 guides.
- **Fixed titles and descriptions** that were too short or too long on 16+ pages; added missing social-share URLs and 3 missing image descriptions.
- **Fixed two compliance risks**: patient quotes that were bypassing the written-consent check now hide until consent is on file; printed QR booking links now point at fmctrichy.com instead of the old preview address.
- Added a launch-gate check so the site can never silently go live with search indexing switched off.
